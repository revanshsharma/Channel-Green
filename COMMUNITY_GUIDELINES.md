# Community Guidelines

These are the house rules inside the world. Short version: be gentle, and look out for each other.

1. *Be yourself, be kind.* Use any name that is not hateful, a real person you are impersonating, or a slur.
2. *Consent first.* Microphone and camera stay off until you choose to turn them on, and nobody can switch them on for you. Do not record other people.
3. *No sexual content.* Keep it all-ages.
4. *No harassment.* If someone asks you to stop, stop. Blocking someone hides them from you straight away.
5. *Confession wall.* It is anonymous to other players, but not to moderators. Posts disappear after a few days. No names, no accusations, no personal details, nothing aimed at one person.
6. *Watch and listen together.* Share only links you are allowed to share. Nothing that breaks the rules above.
7. *Quiet rooms are quiet.* Respect rooms marked quiet.
8. *Report, do not retaliate.* Use the report button. Guides review reports and can mute, remove or ban.

The server filters some words and limits how fast you can chat. This is a safety net, not a replacement for being decent. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line; a pixel room cannot help you with that.
