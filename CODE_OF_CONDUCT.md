# Code of Conduct

Channel Green exists so people can feel they belong. This applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, and the world itself.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome whatever their age, body, background, identity, ability or experience.

## What we do

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Listen, and disagree without contempt.
- Respect boundaries. If someone asks for space, give it.
- Give credit for other people's work.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, hate, threats, or targeting someone for who they are.
- Sexual content or sexual attention toward people who have not asked for it.
- Sharing private information about others.
- Impersonation, spam, scams, or attempts to disrupt the space.
- Encouraging self-harm.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, content or contributions, and may ban people from the project. In the world, guides can mute, remove or ban. Report problems with the in-world report button or by email to the project owners (the address is on https://zerotheoryapp.tech). Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
